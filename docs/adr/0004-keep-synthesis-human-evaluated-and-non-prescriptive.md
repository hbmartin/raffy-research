# Keep synthesis human-evaluated and non-prescriptive

Because the CEO, not the system, makes market decisions, Weekly Reports surface evidence-backed observations, questions, and contradictions without recommendations, confidence claims, or decision-making authority. The Analyst operates the quality loop around generation and after publication: Keep/Junk Source Relevance Labels assess acquisition and exclude junk from future synthesis, while Report Rubric Scores assess relevance, accuracy, and novelty; neither creates a pre-publication approval gate nor changes a frozen report.
